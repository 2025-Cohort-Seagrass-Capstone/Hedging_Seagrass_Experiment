# Hedging_Seagrass_Experiment

**Description:** Statistical analysis of seagrass data for the Hedging Materials for *Thalassia testudinum* Nursery Establishment and *Halophila stipulacea* Management in the Caribbean experimental project performed in Brewers Bay, USVI at the University of the Virgin Islands.

**Collaborators:** Savanna R. Saunders and Adam Krogmann

# Folders

## Brewers_Plots

**Code:** All visual and statistical analysis script files for Brewers plot data

**Figures/Publication_Ready:** All generated figures from Brewers plots script files submitted to journal

**DataFiles:** All excel files used in scripts for visual and statistical analysis.

## Map_Plot_Layout

- Figures created outside of RStudio

**Figure_1.pdf** Map of the study site location at Brewers Bay, St. Thomas, U.S.Virgin Islands (18°20’29.25” N 64°58’38.70” W). The black square indicates the location of hedge placement, while Range Cay (18.340042” N, 64.977267” W) marks the origin of the plugs used in the study.

**Figure_2.pdf** Plot layout outlining the randomization of plots (control, large coral rubble (LCR), burlap bags with small coral rubble (SCR), wine bottles (WB), and glass brick hedges (GB)).

**Figure_3.pdf** Image of in situ hedging types: (A) Large Coral Rubble (LCR), (B) Burlap Bags with Small Coral Rubble (SCR), (C) No Hedging Control, (D) Wine Bottles (WB), (E) Glass Bricks (GB), (F) Random No Hedging Control. Images taken by TG-7 Olympus on the initial outplanting day, February 25, 2026 (A-E) & the final clean-up day, October 9, 2026 (F).
